Tabular foundation models

Closing the Context Gap

Activation Alignment for Tabular In-Context Learning

Yoel Zeldes

Tabular foundation models must carry every training example through the forward pass on every prediction - because at their foundation, they are Transformers performing in-context learning over the entire training set. Shrink the context to go faster and accuracy collapses.

Activation alignment to the rescue: it fits a linear projection - on a CPU, in seconds - so a small-context student approximates the internal representations of a full-context teacher.

2-4× Effective data gain An aligned model with 10% of the data has the performance of an unaligned model with 20-40% of the data

How it works

The frozen model M is evaluated in two regimes: a teacher that sees all training examples, and a lightweight student that sees only a fraction. A simple linear projection is trained to align the student's layer-k activations onto the teacher's activations, using easy to generate unlabeled synthetic queries drawn from the feature distribution.
